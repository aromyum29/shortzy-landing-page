## 4. How it works

1. **Add a video.** Paste a YouTube link or choose a video file from your computer.
   Behind the scenes: downloads the public video or opens your file, and keeps a working copy in your workspace folder.
2. **Pick what you want, see the cost.** Choose 1 to 10 clips, a caption style and, if you like, a topic. Check the estimated AI cost, then press start.
   Behind the scenes: works out a cost range from the video's length, your options and your model. The estimate makes no paid AI call.
3. **Shortzy and your AI find the moments.** They look for a clear hook and a complete payoff. If only six hold up, you get six, not filler.
   Behind the scenes: sends a prepared copy of the audio and video to your Gemini or Qwen account. Back comes an editing plan (timestamps, titles, scores, word timings) that Shortzy checks before using.
4. **Your computer makes the shorts.** Each moment is trimmed, reframed to 9:16 and captioned on your Mac or Windows PC.
   Behind the scenes: bundled video tools and on-device face tracking do the work. Slides get fit framing. No face found? It uses a centred crop and tells you.
5. **Review and export.** Clips arrive titled and ranked, with the reasons each was picked. Download one MP4 or the whole set as a ZIP.
   Behind the scenes: sorts by editorial score and keeps earlier clip sets in your folder. The score explains a pick; it doesn't predict views.

## 8. FAQ

**How much does it cost?**
Shortzy is a one-time purchase, and the price isn't set yet. There's no subscription, because there's no Shortzy cloud to keep paying for, and there are no credits. Your only running cost is your own AI usage, billed by your provider.

**How much will the AI part cost me?**
It depends on the video's length and your model, and Shortzy shows an estimated range before anything runs. One sample in the app: $0.03 to $0.06 for a 24-minute video on Qwen 3.8 Omni Flash. Your provider's bill is final.

**Do I need an AI account?**
Yes, a Gemini or Qwen account with an API key; Kimi is coming soon. Any billing is with that provider, not Shortzy. Never made a key? Setup walks you through it and checks the connection. You can look around first, but you need an AI connected to make clips.

**Does my video get uploaded anywhere?**
Not to Shortzy. To find moments, a prepared copy of the audio and video goes to the AI provider you connected, under their terms. Editing and export happen on your computer, your original is never changed, and your key stays in your system's credential store.

**Is Shortzy a website or an app?**
An app for your Mac (Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later) or Windows PC, with everything it needs bundled in. Start with about 20 GB of disk space. You'll need an internet connection for YouTube imports and AI analysis.

**Will these clips get me views?**
Nobody can promise that, and we won't. Each clip gets an editorial score with reasons, like a strong opening or a complete payoff, so you can decide what to post first.

**Can I clip any YouTube video?**
Shortzy imports public videos, keeps the source attribution and doesn't get around private or sign-in restricted videos. Only clip videos you own or have permission to reuse.
