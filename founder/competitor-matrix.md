# Competitor matrix: how clipping tools charge

Shortzy (pre-launch): Mac and Windows clipping app, one-time purchase (amount not set). The user connects their own AI (Gemini or Qwen today) and pays that provider directly. Editing, captions and exports run locally; AI analysis sends selected media to the chosen provider.

Assumption: the target customer is creators who turn podcasts, interviews and tutorials into shorts (from docs/SHORTZY_LLM_HANDOFF.md).

How this was checked: vendor sites could not be opened directly from this session (network policy). Prices come from the vendors' own pages as returned by web search on 2026-10-09; funding comes from the linked press or tracker. Search copies can lag the live page, so confirm live before quoting. Where a vendor's pages disagree, both are shown.

## 1. Market landscape

**OpusClip** ([opus.pro](https://www.opus.pro/)). Segment: creators to media teams. Says: "#1 AI video clipping tool".
- Funding: $20M round led by SoftBank Vision Fund 2, announced 11 March 2025 ([OpusClip blog](https://www.opus.pro/de-de/blog/opusclip-raises-a-new-round-of-funding-from-softbank-and-launches-opussearch), [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/opusclip-20-million-raised-for-ai-based-video-editing-platform)).
- Charges: subscription plus credits. Free $0, 60 credits a month. Starter $15 a month, monthly billing only, 150 credits. Pro $29 a month or $174 a year, 300 credits a month. Business custom ([pricing](https://www.opus.pro/pricing), checked 2026-10-09). 1 credit = 1 minute of source video imported ([help](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/how-are-credits-consumed)). The help center also names a Max plan; price Not found.
- Running out: monthly-plan credits expire after 60 days. Pro and Max can buy top-ups valid 12 months. Free credits are not refilled. X posts cost 3 credits from 24 Sept 2026 ([help: credits](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/how-are-credits-consumed), [help: plans](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/7285187-credit-related-questions)).
- Free tier: watermarked; clips stop being exportable after 3 days ([pricing](https://www.opus.pro/pricing)).

**Vizard** ([vizard.ai](https://vizard.ai/)). Segment: creators and small teams.
- Funding: Not found.
- Charges: Free $0, 60 credits a month, 720p, 3-day storage. Creator: 600 upload minutes a month, no watermark. Business: priced per seat ([pricing](https://vizard.ai/pricing)). Creator is about $14.50 a month billed annually per Vizard's own blog ([blog](https://vizard.ai/blog/the-12-best-ai-video-editors-in-2025-popularity-value-and-user-experience)); current dollar figures on the pricing page: Not found. 1 credit = 1 minute uploaded ([help](https://help.vizard.ai/en/articles/12017624-how-credits-are-used-for-ai-processing)).
- Running out: upgrade mid-cycle and pay the difference. Credits roll over for 2 months on monthly plans, 13 months on yearly ([help: upgrade](https://help.vizard.ai/en/articles/10441977-how-to-upgrade-or-downgrade-your-plan), [help: plans](https://help.vizard.ai/en/articles/8767574-how-does-the-pricing-plan-work)). Standalone top-ups: Not found.
- Free tier: Vizard watermark; projects expire after 3 days ([help](https://help.vizard.ai/en/articles/8767572-what-does-the-free-plan-for-vizard-offer)).

**Klap** ([klap.app](https://klap.app/)). Segment: individual creators to teams.
- Funding: Not found.
- Charges: clips per month, billed yearly. Basic $14 a month (100 clips), Pro $39 (300), Pro+ $94 (1,000) ([pricing](https://klap.app/pricing)). Klap's blog lists $29, $79 and $189 monthly, with 45 minute to 3 hour length caps; possibly dated ([blog](https://klap.app/blog/opus-clip-pricing)).
- Running out: Not found.
- Free: 1 video, no card. Klap's own pages disagree on whether it is watermarked ([tool page](https://klap.app/tools/ai-clip-generator), [blog](https://klap.app/blog/ai-clip-maker)).

**Submagic** ([submagic.co](https://www.submagic.co/)). Segment: short-form creators; captions first.

- Charges: credits that map to videos, with a length cap. Starter $19 a month (15 videos, 2 min max), Pro $39 (40 videos, 5 min), Business $69 (100 videos, 30 min). Yearly: $12, $23, $41 a month ([pricing](https://www.submagic.co/pricing)). Long-video clipping is an add-on: $19 a month for 10 uploads ([help](https://care.submagic.co/en/article/how-much-does-it-cost-to-use-submagic-1xm83pa/)), "+$12/month annual" on another Submagic page ([page](https://www.submagic.co/ai-caption)).

**Munch** ([getmunch.com](https://www.getmunch.com/)). Segment: brands and agencies.

- Free tier: 720p with a Descript watermark per the help center ([help](https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10255814959245-Export-a-video-or-GIF-locally)); the pricing page lists 1 watermark-free export a month. Conflict.

**Riverside** ([riverside.com](https://riverside.com/)). Segment: podcast and video recording; clips come bundled.

Business custom ([pricing](https://riverside.com/pricing)). A Riverside blog post lists Pro at $35 and says the free plan became a 14-day trial ([blog](https://riverside.com/blog/is-riverside-free)). Conflict.
- Running out: clip cap Not found.

**Adjacent: tools already selling "local" or "bring your own key"**
- OpenShorts: open source (MIT). Self-host free with Docker and your own keys, Gemini first. Hosted cloud from $12 a month for 100 minutes; free cloud tier 20 minutes a month, watermarked ([site](https://www.openshorts.app/), [GitHub](https://github.com/mutonby/openshorts)).
- Clippie V6: Windows desktop app, $39.99 one-time "lifetime license". Lists a "Universal AI Provider" (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Ollama). Whether cloud AI needs the user's own key: Not found ([site](https://clippie.tech/)).
- ClipForge: local-first, Windows 10/11, macOS "coming soon". Local Ollama or your own API key. Subscription: $0, $5, $15, $40 a month ([site](https://videoclipforge.com/)).
- CapCut: editor with AI credits. Pro $19.99 a month or $179.99 a year per a CapCut resource page ([CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/resource/capcut-standard-vs-pro)). Subscription credits last one billing cycle; bought credits last 2 years ([help](https://www.capcut.com/help/credit-types)).

## 3. Positioning gaps

**Gap 1: pay once, your own AI key, on a Mac.** None of the seven cloud tools lists a one-time license or a way to plug in your own key. The tools that do are Windows-only (Clippie, ClipForge) or need Docker (OpenShorts self-host).
- Why it matters: a weekly clipper carries a monthly bill and an allowance that can expire. A Gemini key cannot be used inside these tools.
- Difficulty: medium. Built; signed Mac and Windows installers are still open per the handoff.
- Head start: Estimate: 1 to 3 months against the local tools, since ClipForge already announces macOS. Longer against cloud tools, whose revenue is the subscription and credits, so a pay-once option cuts against their model.

**Gap 2: the cost in money before anything runs.** Competitors meter in credits, minutes or clips, and the rules shift (OpusClip moved X posts from 1 to 3 credits in September 2026). Shortzy shows an estimated provider cost range for the actual video before any paid call; the sample screen shows "$0.03 to $0.06" for a 24 minute video, labelled as an estimate. No money estimate was found on any competitor.
- Why it matters: credit math is this category's friction.
- Difficulty: low, but it only makes sense with your own key.
- Head start: Estimate: 1 to 3 months. Easy to copy for the local tools; cloud tools can show credits, not provider cost.

**Gap 3: bring your own key without developer setup.** OpenShorts self-host runs in Docker and asks for several keys. Shortzy walks the user through choosing a model, seeing the cost, and verifying the key in five steps.
- Difficulty: medium.
- Head start: Estimate: 3 to 6 months. Onboarding polish is slower to copy than a feature, though OpenShorts' paid cloud already skips setup.

## 4. Threat assessment

1. **OpusClip: high.** Most capital ($20M SoftBank-led round). Same job: ranked shorts from long video. Its free 60 credits a month is the hardest comparison for light users. Credit and top-up rules changed in September 2026, so it iterates fast.
2. **OpenShorts: medium.** Same bring-your-own-key pitch on Gemini, free to self-host, with a hosted plan for people who want no setup. Funding: Not found. Its pricing page shows changes dated 23 Sept 2026; changelog not checked.
3. **Clippie: medium to low.** "Pay once, local" at $39.99, but Windows only; key model unclear. Funding and changelog: Not found.

## 5. Strategic recommendations

- **Position to own:** the pay-once clipping app for Mac creators who bring their own Gemini or Qwen key, starting with podcasts and tutorials. Windows next.
- **Feature to ship first:** the per-video cost estimate in money, shown before anything runs, with the guided key connection. It is the proof behind "no credits".
- **Competitor to watch:** OpenShorts. It already runs on Gemini with your own key; a packaged desktop installer would put it in this exact niche. Also watch ClipForge's announced macOS build.

## 6. Category statements for the landing page (rule D3)

Safe, with sources above:
- "Most clipping tools are a monthly or yearly subscription." All seven cloud tools.
- "Many meter you in credits, upload minutes or clips." OpusClip, Vizard, Klap, Submagic, Munch, Descript.
- "On many free plans, exports carry a watermark." OpusClip, Vizard, Submagic, Riverside, Descript help center.
- "Unused credits often expire or reset." OpusClip, Vizard, Descript, CapCut.
- "When the allowance runs out, you upgrade, buy a top-up, or wait." OpusClip, Vizard, Munch, Descript.
- Shortzy: "Pay once. No subscription. No credits. Your AI provider bills you for what you use, at their rates."

Unsafe:
- "Cheaper", "saves you money", "costs less than credits", "pennies per clip". Light users can stay on free tiers.
- "Other tools charge too much" or "overcharge".
- "The only" or "the first" pay-once, local or bring-your-own-key clipper. Clippie, ClipForge and OpenShorts exist.
- "Unlimited clips", or "free for life" without "after the one-time purchase". Every run costs AI usage.
- "No watermark, unlike the others". Paid plans remove it everywhere.
- "Credits always expire". Not true for every tool or credit type.
- Competitor names or prices on the page.
- "Works with any AI", "offline", "nothing leaves your computer".
