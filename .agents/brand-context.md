## Brand
- **Name**: Shortzy (always with a z; the wordmark artwork is lowercase)
- **Category**: Desktop clipping app (long-form video to short-form clips), sold as a one-time purchase
- **Description**: A clipping app for Mac and Windows. Add your own video or a YouTube link; Shortzy's clipping logic and the creator's own AI (Gemini or Qwen) find the strongest moments, and the computer edits them into titled, captioned 9:16 shorts. Pay once for Shortzy, pay your AI provider as you go.
- **Tagline**: Long video in. Shorts out. (brand tagline; the hero leads with "Turn long videos into shorts. Pay once, it's yours.")
- **Stage**: Pre-launch wishlist. Marketed for Mac (Apple Silicon, macOS 14+) and Windows (owner decision, 9 Oct 2026). Windows is a build target, not yet a verified build, so never claim feature parity.
- **Website**: This landing page (domain to be confirmed)

## Pricing model
- **Shortzy**: a one-time purchase. No subscription, no credits, no allowance that resets. The amount is not set, so no price is shown anywhere. "Pay once" and "Pay once, it's yours" are fine; never call Shortzy free.
- **AI usage**: billed by the user's own provider (Google for Gemini, Alibaba Cloud for Qwen), at their rates, with no markup from Shortzy. Shortzy shows an estimated range before each run; checking it makes no paid AI call.
- **Sample costs on the page** (real app fixtures, always with the model named and the estimate and provider-bill qualifiers): $0.43 to $1.33 for a 44:48 video on Gemini Flash 3.8 (recommended; recorded usage $0.43), and $0.03 to $0.06 for a 24-minute video on Qwen 3.8 Omni Flash.
- **To confirm with the founder**: whether the purchase includes updates, covers both Mac and Windows, and how many computers.

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Creators who already publish long-form video and want a steady short-form presence: podcasters and interviewers, educators and tutorial makers, business and AI explainers. Many already pay a monthly clipping plan with credits.
- **Key Problem**: Getting shorts out of a long video means hours of editing by hand, or another monthly bill with a credit meter that runs out, renews whether they post or not, and keeps their projects in someone else's cloud.
- **Their Language** *(assumption, from category norms)*: "repurpose", "clips", "shorts", "find the good bits", "captions", "reframe to vertical", "credits ran out", "another subscription", "I don't have time to edit".
- **Technical comfort**: Comfortable with everyday tech, not with APIs, model names or tokens. Say "your AI account" above the fold, label the field accurately as an API key, reveal detail progressively.

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**:
  1. Pay once. A one-time purchase in a category of monthly plans and credits. A month you don't clip costs nothing in Shortzy fees.
  2. Your own AI, paid as you go. Users connect their own Gemini or Qwen account (Kimi coming soon) and pay the provider directly. No credit packs, no markup. The cost estimate is shown before anything runs.
  3. Edited on your computer. Trimming, framing (on-device face tracking), captions and export run locally; projects and originals stay in a folder you choose.
  4. Your video or a YouTube link in; honest output out. Asks for 10, finds 6 strong moments, returns 6 and says why. Scores are editorial estimates with visible reasons, never a views guarantee.
- **Competitors**: cloud clip generators sold as subscriptions with credits, minutes or clip allowances, plus manual editing in an editor. Sourced details in `founder/competitor-matrix.md`. Never name them on the page.
- **Market Position**: Niche, creator-first, ownership over renting, value-transparent. Never claim to be cheaper or to save money.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: warm, direct, playful, honest, capable
- **Tone**: casual but not sloppy; playful in visuals (filmstrip mascot), plain and specific in claims
- **Voice Admires** *(assumption)*: Things, Panic, Raycast, early Notion

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: Creator control and ownership, honesty over hype, privacy by default, respect for people's time
- **Mission**: Help creators turn the long videos they already make into shorts worth posting, with software they own and an AI bill they control.

## Visual Identity (from brand pack v3.2)

- Radii: 12px controls, 24px cards, pills fully rounded. Focus: 3px maroon ring, 4px offset (paper ring on dark).

## Hard rules (from the owner)
- Never imply a clip score guarantees views.
- "Local" is not "offline": a prepared copy of the audio and video goes to the user's chosen AI provider for analysis, and YouTube imports and AI analysis need an internet connection. Never imply that nothing leaves the computer; where the page explains how it works or what stays private, say so.
- Never call Shortzy free; it is paid once. "Costs nothing" only for the estimate or for no Shortzy fees after the purchase.
- Do not promise savings versus competitors, and do not name competitors on the page.
- No price amount until the founder sets one.
- Do not call the product production-ready.
- Do not use em dashes or en dashes in product copy.
- Page copy uses American spelling (color, centered, favorite, customize), matching the app UI.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: Wishlist signups ahead of launch, from creators who want to own their clipping tool (no trial and no price amount shown yet)
- **Secondary Goal**: Make the pricing model understood in one read: pay once for Shortzy, pay your AI as you go
- **Key Metrics** *(assumption)*: wishlist signups, "See how it works" engagement, FAQ opens on the cost questions
