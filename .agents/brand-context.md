## Brand
- **Name**: Shortzy (always with a z; the wordmark artwork is lowercase)
- **Category**: Desktop video-repurposing app (long-form video to short-form clips)
- **Description**: A Mac app that finds the strongest moments in a long video and turns them into titled, captioned 9:16 shorts, using the creator's own AI key.
- **Tagline**: Long video in. Shorts out.
- **Stage**: Pre-launch. Private QA build on macOS (Apple Silicon, macOS 14+). Windows is a target, not shipping.
- **Website**: This landing page (domain to be confirmed)

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Creators who already publish long-form video and want a steady short-form presence: podcasters and interviewers, educators and tutorial makers, business and AI explainers.
- **Key Problem**: Turning an hour of good material into shorts means scrubbing, cutting, reframing, captioning and exporting by hand, over and over. Most of them have the long videos; they lack the hours.
- **Their Language** *(assumption, from category norms)*: "repurpose", "clips", "shorts", "find the good bits", "captions", "reframe to vertical", "I don't have time to edit", "posting consistently".
- **Technical comfort**: Comfortable with everyday tech, not with APIs, model names or tokens. Explain in plain language, reveal detail progressively.

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**:
  1. Runs on your Mac. Editing, framing, captions and export happen locally; projects and originals stay in a folder you choose.
  2. Bring your own AI. Users connect their own Gemini or Qwen key and pay the provider directly. No credit packs, no markup.
  3. Honest output. Asks for 10, finds 6 strong moments, returns 6 and says why. Scores are editorial estimates with visible reasons, never a views guarantee.
  4. Cost shown before anything runs.
- **Competitors** *(assumption)*: Cloud clip generators such as Opus Clip, Vizard, Klap and Munch; manual editing in CapCut, Premiere or Descript.
- **Market Position**: Niche, creator-first, value-transparent.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: warm, direct, playful, honest, capable
- **Tone**: casual but not sloppy; playful in visuals (filmstrip mascot), plain and specific in claims
- **Voice Admires** *(assumption)*: Things, Panic, Raycast, early Notion

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: Creator control, honesty over hype, privacy by default, respect for people's time
- **Mission**: Help creators turn the long videos they already make into shorts worth posting, without handing over their files or their budget.

## Visual Identity (from brand pack v3.2)
- Palette: maroon #702F42, deep maroon #43202B, warm paper #F3F1EE, white, ink #292628, muted #625B60, pebble #C9C5C1, coral #E7ADA0, rose clay #E9D6D2, oat gold #D5C28F
- Composition: ~70% neutral surfaces, 20% maroon, 10% accents. Matte: no gloss, glow, bevel or metallic effects.
- Radii: 12px controls, 24px cards, pills fully rounded. Focus: 3px maroon ring, 4px offset (paper ring on dark).
- Mascot: maroon filmstrip character, four poses (welcome, thinking, clipping, celebration). One mascot per panel.

## Hard rules (from the owner)
- Never imply a clip score guarantees views.
- "Local-first" is not "offline": selected audio, video, frames or transcript go to the user's chosen AI provider for analysis.
- Do not promise universal savings versus competitors.
- Do not call the product production-ready or claim Windows support.
- Do not use em dashes in product copy.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: 7-day free trial starts from the landing page *(assumption: trial requires the download)*
- **Key Metrics** *(assumption)*: trial starts, "See how it works" engagement, trial-to-paid conversion
